
## Linux Brocade FC/FCOE 适配器驱

### 支持的硬

bfa 3.0.2.2 驱动支持所Brocade FC/FCOE 适配器。以下是各适配器型号及对应 PCIID 的列表
	===================	===========================================
	PCIID			型号
	===================	===========================================
	1657:0013:1657:0014	425 4Gbps 双端FC HBA
	1657:0013:1657:0014	825 8Gbps PCIe 双端FC HBA
	1657:0013:103c:1742	HP 82B 8Gbps PCIe 双端FC HBA
	1657:0013:103c:1744	HP 42B 4Gbps 双端FC HBA
	1657:0017:1657:0014	415 4Gbps 单端FC HBA
	1657:0017:1657:0014	815 8Gbps 单端FC HBA
	1657:0017:103c:1741	HP 41B 4Gbps 单端FC HBA
	1657:0017:103c 1743	HP 81B 8Gbps 单端FC HBA
	1657:0021:103c:1779	804 8Gbps FC HBA（用HP Bladesystem c-class
	1657:0014:1657:0014	1010 10Gbps 单端CNA - FCOE
	1657:0014:1657:0014	1020 10Gbps 双端CNA - FCOE
	1657:0014:1657:0014	1007 10Gbps 双端CNA - FCOE
	1657:0014:1657:0014	1741 10Gbps 双端CNA - FCOE

	1657:0022:1657:0024	1860 16Gbps FC HBA
	1657:0022:1657:0022	1860 10Gbps CNA - FCOE
	===================	===========================================
